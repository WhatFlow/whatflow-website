If the important part is sending under your own business identity and handling replies in Shopify, I’d start with WhatFlow Business API. The official connection, team inbox and native Flow actions are available from Free, with no seat charges.

## What does wa (wava) do well?

The wa listing, by wava, advertises abandoned checkout recovery, COD confirmations, shipping updates and a storefront button. Its AI agent can answer product and FAQ questions; the app also advertises continued use of the WhatsApp Business app on your number. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/wava-abandoned-cart-recovery).

## What does the setup cost?

The free-to-install tier lists one reminder using a shared wava number, with automated rates starting at $0.01 per message and varying by country. Lite is $17.99/month and lists own-number sending, two reminders, a team inbox, Flow actions and 100 AI replies per month.

At 800 sends in a billing cycle, WhatFlow Starter is $4.99 in app fees before Meta charges and tax. Compare that with wava’s own-number Lite plan rather than treating its shared-number free install as the same setup. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Imagine a shopper receives a recovery link at lunch and asks a sizing question before paying. Test whose name appears on the message, where the reply lands and whether your staff can find the checkout without asking for the same details again.

![WhatFlow template action with Shopify checkout fields in Flow](/api/media/file/blog-shopify-flow-whatflow-flow-template-mapping.jpg "An inactive demo workflow maps Shopify data into a WhatsApp template.")

Get the total for your country and selected sender setup; include Meta charges where applicable and keep any AI reply allowance separate from message volume.

## Which one would I choose?

wava’s paid tier is worth comparing if an AI product agent or keeping the existing Business app in use is central to the rollout. WhatFlow has no built-in AI reply bot.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
