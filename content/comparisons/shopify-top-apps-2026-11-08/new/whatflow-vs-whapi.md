Both products have a route to a multi-agent inbox without buying one license per agent. WhatFlow’s advantage is that the inbox, catalog sharing and native Shopify Flow are available on Free, so you can start a staff pilot without paying for a Team upgrade.

## What does Whapi do well?

Whapi’s Shopify listing describes official WhatsApp API sending, COD confirmations, shipping updates, review requests and auto-tagging. It also advertises connecting the WhatsApp Business app and seeing chats. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/whapi-whatsapp).

## What does the setup cost?

Pro is $18.99/month or $149/year and lists WhatsApp API charges separately. Team is $45/month or $299/year and adds multiple accounts, a shared inbox with unlimited agents, automatic assignment and agent metrics.

If you need a staffed inbox, Whapi’s $45 Team plan is the monthly benchmark. WhatFlow can pilot the team on Free. At 1,200 sends in a billing cycle, WhatFlow Starter is $4.99 before Meta fees and tax; compare multiple-account needs separately. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Picture a support lead hiring a seasonal picker to help confirm deliveries for two weeks. Give the helper an inbox login and test ownership without changing the billing plan. Check who can access the connected number and how the lead reviews assigned threads.

![WhatFlow inbox assignment filters](/api/media/file/blog-product-proof-inbox-assignment-filters.jpg "Demo inbox showing assignment filters.")

Price the right plan for the number of accounts and agents, then add Meta charges for the template mix you actually send.

## Which one would I choose?

Whapi’s Team plan merits a look when multiple WhatsApp accounts and its specific agent reporting are hard requirements. Ask for a demo with your account structure instead of assuming every inbox handles it the same way.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
