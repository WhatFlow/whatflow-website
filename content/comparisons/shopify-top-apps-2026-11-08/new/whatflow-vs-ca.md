If you want the buyer to complete Shopify checkout, I’d choose WhatFlow. The theme button gives them a place to ask questions, and the official API, Flow actions and team inbox handle what happens once an eligible checkout or order exists.

## What does CA WhatsApp Chat Order Button do well?

CA focuses on letting shoppers start a WhatsApp order from a product or cart page. Its listing mentions per-product numbers, click analytics, server-side Meta Pixel events and automated checkout recovery on a paid tier. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/ca-chat-order-button-widget).

## What does the setup cost?

The free plan lists a floating widget and manual order and checkout follow-up allowances. Advanced is $4.99/month and adds product/cart order buttons and advanced tracking. Pro at $9.99/month lists 1,000 monthly automation messages.

Price the click-to-order controls and the automated sends separately. CA’s Advanced tier adds the ordering controls; Pro lists automation. WhatFlow’s paid tier increases message allowance and doesn’t add a separate charge for the team inbox. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Say you sell a made-to-measure rug and need the customer to send dimensions before you take payment. An order-in-chat button may be the main feature. For ready-to-ship stock, use chat to resolve the question and leave payment and order creation in the normal checkout.

![WhatFlow template action with Shopify checkout fields in Flow](/api/media/file/blog-shopify-flow-whatflow-flow-template-mapping.jpg "An inactive demo workflow maps Shopify data into a WhatsApp template.")

Decide where the order is created, who answers the conversation and which events count as a conversion before turning on automated follow-up.

## Which one would I choose?

CA is a credible pick when product-specific WhatsApp ordering or measuring button clicks with Meta is the project. Don’t compare CA’s Advanced subscription to the WhatFlow messaging allowance as though they buy the same workflow.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
