I’d use WhatFlow when several people cover the same store number. A theme-matched button gets the shopper into WhatsApp, while inbox assignment lets the team decide who answers instead of asking the shopper to pick a department.

## What does SeedGrow WhatsApp Chat Widget do well?

SeedGrow gives shoppers a floating widget or product-page click-to-chat button, and can display multiple WhatsApp numbers. Its Pro tier adds agent working hours, offline hiding, styling and click reporting. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/click-to-chat).

## What does the setup cost?

The free tier lists page visibility controls, multiple numbers and prefilled messages. Pro is $6.90/month and lists Google Analytics event reporting, working hours and expanded styling.

Two department numbers can already fit SeedGrow’s free widget tier. Paying for Pro makes sense if the schedule and click reporting matter. With WhatFlow, the paid plan increases sending allowance; staff assignments and the theme button don’t require an upgrade. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Imagine a customer picks “Sales” because they bought a jacket yesterday, but really needs help with its shipment. When teams use separate numbers, ownership can get awkward. In a single inbox, staff can assign the thread to the person who can check the order.

![WhatFlow inbox assignment filters](/api/media/file/blog-product-proof-inbox-assignment-filters.jpg "Demo inbox showing assignment filters.")

Try the product page on mobile and test a message outside staff hours. Decide whether the customer should select a number or the team should assign the conversation.

## Which one would I choose?

SeedGrow fits when departments intentionally use different numbers and the goal is to expose those numbers cleanly. WhatFlow fits when you want the Shopify workflows and a shared queue behind a single entry point.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
