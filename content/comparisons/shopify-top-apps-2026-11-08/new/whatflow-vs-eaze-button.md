For WhatsApp-first stores I’d start with the WhatFlow theme button. It’s free forever, and if you later need a shared inbox, shipping updates or checkout recovery, those are in the same Shopify app.

## What does EAZE Button do well?

EAZE is a direct-message button with prefilled text, device visibility and appearance controls. Its paid tier lists business hours, localized button text, custom styling and Google Analytics and Meta Pixel integrations. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/eaze-chat-button).

## What does the setup cost?

Its Shopify listing shows a free tier and a $0.89/month Pro plan. That’s a low price for a specialized storefront button; compare it to a button, not to a full messaging bill.

EAZE Pro costs less than WhatFlow Starter, and that’s fine: one buys expanded button controls, the other buys a higher send allowance. For a shop that just wants a theme button, test both free options before paying for anything. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Suppose you sell kitchenware in two languages. Try a localized button label, then see what happens when the buyer asks for an order update. If all you need is the first click, EAZE can do the job. If support owns the next three messages, test the inbox too.

![WhatFlow theme editor showing WhatsApp button controls](/api/media/file/blog-product-proof-theme-button-controls.jpg "WhatsApp button settings in the WhatFlow demo store.")

Verify page visibility, prefilled text and mobile layout. Then test who receives replies and whether a subsequent order update is handled in the same process.

## Which one would I choose?

I’d choose EAZE if its button styling, localization or tracking controls are your priority. I’d choose WhatFlow when the goal is a conversation tied to order work rather than a link to a separate messaging app.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
