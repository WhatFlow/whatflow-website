I’d pay attention to the work after the first reminder. WhatFlow gives your Shopify team a shared inbox and native Flow triggers and actions on the free plan, with paid allowances available as sending grows.

## What does Moose WhatsApp Chat Button do well?

Moose’s Shopify listing goes beyond the widget in its name. It describes a WhatsApp inbox, a Meta Business Manager connection, abandoned-cart reminders and button click tracking through Google Analytics and Meta Pixel. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/moose-whatsapp-chat-button).

## What does the setup cost?

Shopify lists Moose as free. That is a real price advantage for the app itself. Check what Meta charges apply to your connected number and whether the advertised features cover the workflow you need.

Moose’s $0 listing is the right baseline. If the WhatFlow workflow needs 300 sends in a cycle, its Free plan app charge would be $2 in overages, before Meta fees and tax. I’d judge that small difference against the time saved by a repeatable order workflow. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Say someone opens WhatsApp from a product page and asks if a replacement filter fits their machine. A widget gets you the conversation. If the same customer later replies to a COD confirmation, test whether that reply can route to fulfillment and update the right order without someone copying it by hand.

![WhatFlow theme editor showing WhatsApp button controls](/api/media/file/blog-product-proof-theme-button-controls.jpg "WhatsApp button settings in the WhatFlow demo store.")

In a demo order, test the customer reply, the Shopify order change and the inbox owner. Check the message bill separately from the app subscription.

## Which one would I choose?

Moose is worth trying if a free customizable widget, checkout reminders and click tracking are the whole brief. WhatFlow is my pick when order follow-up needs to be repeatable across staff.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
