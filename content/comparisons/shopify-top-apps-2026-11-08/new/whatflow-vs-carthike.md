WhatFlow is where I’d start when the checkout queue is already a recurring task. You can set up automatic reminders, templates and a staffed inbox on Free, then change the allowance as volume grows.

## What does CK (Carthike) do well?

CK advertises checkout reminders, COD verification, shipping updates and review requests for services including Judge.me and Loox. The listing also offers a customizable chat button and connection to an existing WhatsApp Business app number. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/carthike-recovery).

## What does the setup cost?

Free Forever includes a chat button and 100 manual cart abandonment messages. VIP at $9.99/month adds automatic recovery, order and review messages. Advanced at $29/month lists custom templates, automation from your own number, and a team inbox with 1,000 incoming messages. Listed automated rates start at $0.02 per message and vary by country.

If you plan to send 600 automated reminders a month, CK’s free manual allowance isn’t the right baseline. Price its VIP automation and any per-message fees; WhatFlow Starter includes that volume in its app allowance, with Meta charges still separate. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

Say your team follows up manually every Friday and sees the same shoppers again on Monday. Trial a timed reminder on an opted-in checkout and make sure a completed purchase exits the reminder path. Then assign any reply to someone who can resolve it.

![WhatFlow template action with Shopify checkout fields in Flow](/api/media/file/blog-shopify-flow-whatflow-flow-template-mapping.jpg "An inactive demo workflow maps Shopify data into a WhatsApp template.")

Compare manual sends, automated sends and incoming inbox messages separately. The plans meter different things, so a single per-message number won’t settle the choice.

## Which one would I choose?

CK’s free manual workflow is a sensible starting point if you want to read each checkout before sending, and its named review integrations are worth testing if they matter to your stack.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
