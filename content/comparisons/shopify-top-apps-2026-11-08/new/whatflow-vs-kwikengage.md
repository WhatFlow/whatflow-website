If your first priority is reliable ownership of WhatsApp replies and Shopify order automation, I’d start with WhatFlow. You can test the official API, the team inbox and native Flow blocks on Free, then budget for messages instead of a larger regional package.

## What does KwikEngage do well?

GoKwik’s KwikEngage lists official WhatsApp API access, a unified inbox, a no-code bot builder, advanced targeting and retargeting across additional channels. It aims at teams running several parts of acquisition and support together. [See the Shopify listing](https://apps.shopify.com/kwikengage).

## What does the setup cost?

The Shopify listing shows Growth – India at $40/month and Pro – UK, EU, RoW at $270/month, each with a 14-day trial and additional channel usage charges. These are region-specific packages, not two interchangeable quotes.

For an India store, use the $40 Growth quote in the worksheet. A UK store should use the regional Pro quote instead. In either case, compare the bundle against a WhatFlow app plan plus applicable Meta delivery fees, and count only the extra channels your staff will actually use. Check [current WhatFlow pricing](/pricing) for allowances and overage rates.

## How would I test it in a Shopify store?

A COD brand might want to tag a confirmed order, pause an uncertain one and hand an address question to support. Prove that workflow before paying for wider retargeting. If your team will also use email, SMS and an AI helpdesk every day, price that wider scope on its own merits.

![WhatFlow messaging actions in Shopify Flow](/api/media/file/blog-shopify-flow-whatflow-flow-actions-more.jpg "WhatFlow actions available from the Shopify Flow editor.")

Request the plan for your billing region, list the channels you will actually use and estimate each channel’s charges for a normal month.

## Which one would I choose?

KwikEngage is worth a pilot for a team buying omnichannel retargeting, bot tools and a larger marketing setup. WhatFlow is my choice for a store whose day-to-day work starts in Shopify and ends on WhatsApp.

For WhatFlow in this comparison, I mean the [official Cloud API app](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api). I’d pilot it on Free with the [shared inbox](/inbox) and the Shopify Flow actions you expect to use. Once the customer journey works, size the messaging plan from the sends you actually expect.
