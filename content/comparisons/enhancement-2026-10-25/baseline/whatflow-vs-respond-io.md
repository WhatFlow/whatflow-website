## A realistic Shopify scenario

A sales organization wants lead routing across several channels, while the store team mainly needs shipping and cart conversations. Identify which responsibility is driving the purchase before paying for the larger operating model.

## Where respond.io focuses

Respond.io describes a platform that brings customer touchpoints, CRMs and AI agents into a team inbox. Its site presents lead capture, qualification, routing, bookings and ongoing conversations across channels. That makes it relevant when sales and support staff need to manage a continuing customer relationship.

Confirm the specific channels, integrations and account allowances in the proposed plan. A broad conversation platform can support many designs, but your store still needs a defined implementation.

## Where WhatFlow starts

The WhatFlow demo app organizes automations around Shopify events. The template editor maps fields such as customer name, order total and checkout link into an outgoing message. This gives a store operator a concrete place to maintain an order update or a recovery reminder.

That approach should be judged on its event coverage and clarity, rather than on whether it resembles a full sales CRM. Equally, a conversation platform should be judged on the lead workflow your team actually needs.

## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 40,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Scale produces an app-side cost of $49.99 at that volume. This calculation uses its $49.99 base, 40,000 included sends and $0.002 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
