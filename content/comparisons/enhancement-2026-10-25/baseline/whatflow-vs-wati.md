For a concrete baseline, consider 1,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Starter produces an app-side cost of $4.99 at that volume. This calculation uses its $4.99 base, 2,000 included sends and $0.005 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services.
