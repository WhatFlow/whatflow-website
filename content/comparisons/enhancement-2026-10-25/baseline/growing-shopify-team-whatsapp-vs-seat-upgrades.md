## A controlled test before launch

Invite the expected operators during a pilot, including someone who only helps during sales. Test who can understand the order, continue the conversation and complete a handover.

Write down the intended audience, trigger, message and owner before enabling the workflow. Use test recipients first and include an awkward case, not only the ideal purchase. When the buyer replies, ask another teammate to continue the conversation. The result should be understandable without relying on the first person’s memory. This is a suggested evaluation method, not a claim that we measured a particular uplift in another store.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules.
