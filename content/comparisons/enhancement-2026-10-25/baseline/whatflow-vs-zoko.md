## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 1,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Starter produces an app-side cost of $4.99 at that volume. This calculation uses its $4.99 base, 2,000 included sends and $0.005 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
