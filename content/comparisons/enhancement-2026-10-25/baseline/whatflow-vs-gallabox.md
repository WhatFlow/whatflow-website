## A realistic Shopify scenario

A business captures enquiries before a purchase and passes them to a sales representative. Compare that lead process separately from the Shopify order-confirmation process so that one does not hide gaps in the other.

## What Gallabox describes

Gallabox's website presents a customer journey spanning inquiry capture, qualification, nurturing, conversion and retention. Its examples include WhatsApp, Instagram, web chat and voice, with AI agents involved in lead handling. That makes it relevant to a business with a conversational sales process across entry points.

Ask for the actual setup proposed for your use case. A broad customer journey can depend on integrations, account configuration and internal process design that should be included in the implementation plan.

## Feature access and the people who use the inbox

We built WhatFlow so the first plan does not feel like an intentionally incomplete product. Every feature is available whether the store chooses Free or the highest paid plan. The subscription choice changes messaging allowances and rates, not access to the inbox, Shopify integrations or other features. A merchant can start with one workflow and add another without purchasing a new feature edition.

The team inbox has no seat fees. If the founder, a support colleague and someone in fulfillment all need to participate, adding those people does not create a per-user WhatFlow subscription. That is particularly useful when store staff share responsibilities rather than operating a large formal support department.

## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 20,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
