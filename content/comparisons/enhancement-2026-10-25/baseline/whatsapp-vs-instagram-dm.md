## Start with the customer’s job

Instagram is a natural place for product discovery. A customer may first ask about an item under a post or through a direct message. Once the conversation becomes an order, WhatsApp can provide a useful channel for the store’s ongoing operational messages and replies.

## Where each approach helps

Instagram DMs retain the context of social discovery and can be excellent for creator-led selling. WhatsApp can be more convenient for order follow-up among customers who prefer it. Do not move the customer without permission or treat a social follow as WhatsApp opt-in.

## The practical cost difference

## A controlled test before launch

Follow a product enquiry through checkout and fulfillment. Decide when the customer should be offered WhatsApp and how the team links the resulting conversation to the correct order without losing context.

Write down the intended audience, trigger, message and owner before enabling the workflow. Use test recipients first and include an awkward case, not only the ideal purchase. When the buyer replies, ask another teammate to continue the conversation. The result should be understandable without relying on the first person’s memory. This is a suggested evaluation method, not a claim that we measured a particular uplift in another store.
