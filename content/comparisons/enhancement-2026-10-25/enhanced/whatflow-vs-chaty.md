## Test the least-used contact destination

Hypothetical example: a widget offers WhatsApp, email and phone, but the store usually answers only WhatsApp. Ask a colleague to use the email destination outside the founder’s usual workflow, then identify the receiving system, response expectation and escalation route. Repeat for the phone option rather than evaluating only the familiar WhatsApp tap.

Remove or repair a destination the team cannot support before adding more choices. Chaty’s configurable channel menu can be useful when those routes are staffed. If the intended task is WhatsApp with Shopify context, compare WhatFlow Business API’s free button and downstream conversation workflow.
