## Test continuity without a private explanation

Hypothetical example: a colleague is investigating a missing parcel when their shift ends. Give the receiving operator access to the supported conversation record and the order reference, then ask them to state what was checked and what happens next. Keep the first operator offline during the exercise so an informal phone call cannot hide a missing handoff.

If the second person needs a screenshot from a personal device, identify the information or access gap rather than simply adding another chat group. Record who owns the next carrier check and customer response. WhatFlow Business API’s no-seat-fee inbox makes involving the colleague easier to budget, but the handoff still needs a maintained process.

Run a handover where the first operator goes offline before the problem is resolved. Ask the next person to identify the customer’s request and the next order action without a private explanation.

Compare [inbox and ticket workflows](https://whatflow.tech/compare/whatsapp-inbox-vs-helpdesk-tickets) if unresolved cases need a broader service process.
