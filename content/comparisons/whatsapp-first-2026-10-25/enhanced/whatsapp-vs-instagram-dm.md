## Start with the customer’s job

Instagram is a natural place for product discovery. A customer may first ask about an item under a post or through a direct message. Once the conversation becomes an order, WhatsApp can provide a useful channel for the store’s ongoing operational messages and replies.

## Where each approach helps

Instagram DMs retain the context of social discovery and can be excellent for creator-led selling. WhatsApp can be more convenient for order follow-up among customers who prefer it. Do not move the customer without permission or treat a social follow as WhatsApp opt-in.

## The practical cost difference

Compare the tools needed to manage social leads with the tools needed to handle Shopify events. Paying for a social automation system may be justified for acquisition, but it should not be assumed to cover the entire post-purchase workflow.
