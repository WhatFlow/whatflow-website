## Start with the customer’s job

Not every customer question needs a formal service case. A short delivery clarification may be easiest to handle as a conversation linked to the store’s work. The team still needs ownership and context, but it may not need a larger service-suite purchase.

## Where each approach helps

Ticketing is valuable for complex cases, service-level processes and cross-channel reporting. A conversational inbox is attractive for quick ongoing exchanges. The right choice depends on the complexity of the work, not on whether one interface feels newer.

## Why I would use WhatFlow for this workflow

Keep the quick order exchange in WhatFlow with a named owner and next action. Define the handoff to a ticketing process for a multi-day dispute or formal investigation. That gives the customer a conversational route while preserving the records required for a more complex case.

## Choose the record needed for an unresolved case

For a hypothetical support review, compare a simple delivery clarification with a replacement request that remains open over several days. Define what must survive the handover: the order reference, the next promised action, the responsible person and the evidence supporting the decision. Ask whether the current inbox provides a dependable record for that case or whether the store needs formal ticket processes. WhatFlow can serve the focused Shopify WhatsApp conversation without adding inbox seat fees. A helpdesk may still earn its cost when escalation and cross-channel reporting are required. Keep both requirements visible rather than selecting an interface solely because the first short exchange looked easier.

## WhatFlow’s allowance and team costs

For a concrete baseline, consider 20,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
