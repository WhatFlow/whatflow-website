## Start with the customer’s job

A WhatsApp Channel can distribute public updates to followers. A Shopify order message is a different job: it belongs to a particular customer and may need a reply. Confusing the two can lead a merchant to choose a broadcast surface when the real requirement is private service.

## Where each approach helps

Channels are suited to one-way public updates and audience communication. The Business Platform supports business messaging workflows under its applicable rules. A public update should never be used as a place to expose an individual customer’s order information.

## Classify information before choosing its destination

Prepare a hypothetical collection launch with separate public and customer-specific information. Put product availability, a general launch announcement and an individual shipment problem on separate cards. Ask the team to identify which audience should receive each item before choosing the channel. A follower update can announce the collection; a private order question needs an appropriately configured customer conversation. Do not copy a public follower list into a private campaign or infer permission from following alone. WhatFlow serves the Shopify messaging and inbox side of that distinction. Keep the public announcement useful on its own, with a clear route for someone who voluntarily wants private help about an order.

Write one public collection announcement and one private delivery update. Identify the audience and data for each. The announcement may belong in a Channel; the order detail belongs in an appropriately configured private workflow.
