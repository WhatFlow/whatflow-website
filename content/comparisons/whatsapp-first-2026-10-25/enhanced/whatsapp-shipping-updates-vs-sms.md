## Start with the customer’s job

A tracking link is useful, but a shipping conversation often continues with a question about timing or an address. WhatsApp gives the customer a place to ask that follow-up while keeping the update and reply together.

## Where each approach helps

SMS remains useful for broad carrier-based reach. WhatsApp can be preferable when customers already use it and benefit from richer context or ongoing support. Availability should be checked against the store’s real destination mix.

## Review what happens when tracking is replaced

For a hypothetical shipping exception, let a carrier tracking destination change after the first notification is prepared. Ask the operator which record supplies the new value, whether a correction is needed and how a buyer’s reply is associated with the right parcel. Review the final localized SMS for segments and inspect the WhatsApp template’s event and destination. WhatFlow can keep the WhatsApp question with the team, but it does not make carrier data accurate. A useful comparison includes the stale-link case as well as a clean tracking update.
