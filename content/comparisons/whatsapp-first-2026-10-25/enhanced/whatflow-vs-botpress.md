## The decision behind this comparison

Botpress is a platform for building AI agents, including agents deployed through messaging channels. It gives builders control over agent behavior and integrations. That is a different starting point from installing a Shopify application with existing store workflows.

## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 20,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.

## Separate answering a question from changing an order

Hypothetical example: an agent can explain a published return policy, but a customer then asks it to cancel a specific order. Define which identity and order checks the workflow needs before any state change, and what happens when those checks cannot resolve the record. A fluent response should not count as completed cancellation.

Review the human handoff and the usage unit recorded for a conversation spanning automated and staff assistance. Botpress can be appropriate for that custom behavior; WhatFlow Business API can cover a narrower event-and-reply workflow. Choose the scope before comparing rates, since an AI conversation allowance and a plan’s sent-message allowance are different measures.

### How should I compare Botpress pricing with WhatFlow?

Botpress has a free offering and tiered packages with conversation, seat and agent allowances. Its Team package advertises unlimited seats, so it would be wrong to describe every Botpress deployment as charging per user. Include AI spending and integration work as well as the selected platform package.

### Does WhatFlow charge per inbox seat?

No. WhatFlow’s team inbox has no seat fees. Adding colleagues does not create a per-user subscription; the messaging plan and actual usage still apply.

### Are WhatFlow features restricted on the free plan?

No. All plans have the same feature access. Message allowances and rates differ, and applicable Meta delivery charges are separate. Theme-matched WhatsApp buttons are free forever.
