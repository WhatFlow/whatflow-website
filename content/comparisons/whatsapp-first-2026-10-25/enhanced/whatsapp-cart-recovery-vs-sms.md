## Budget the final text after localization

Take a hypothetical recovery reminder in the store’s main language and a localized version containing the intended punctuation and product name. Ask the SMS provider to calculate the actual segments rather than estimating from an English draft, and inspect the supported reply destination. On the WhatsApp side, check the eligible template, destination and WhatFlow allowance separately. Keep the offer and audience criteria comparable where possible, while documenting buyers reachable through only one channel. A richer WhatsApp conversation may help a sizing question; SMS may be the useful route for another buyer. Decide from observed handling and billed units rather than an assumed worldwide recovery-rate advantage.

Test the same cart and offer with controlled recipients, including non-Latin text if relevant. Confirm checkout links, completed-order suppression and the staff response when the buyer asks for help.
