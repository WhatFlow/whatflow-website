## Start with the customer’s job

The person answering WhatsApp changes as a store grows. A founder may begin alone, then bring in support, fulfillment and a seasonal colleague. Software costs should be reviewed against that real staffing pattern rather than a one-person demo.

## Where each approach helps

## Rehearse the next hire before buying access

Hypothetical example: a founder adds a support colleague and asks a warehouse operator to help during launches. Write down which person reads messages, checks order facts and makes exceptions. Ask each to complete a controlled handoff using the proposed access model. Do not assume all three need identical permissions merely because all can contribute.

Record the subscription change for that staffing pattern separately from any increase in sends. WhatFlow Business API adds no inbox seat fee, while an alternative may include the necessary people in a bundle. The decision should preserve appropriate access and clear responsibility, rather than rewarding either product for an unused capability.

Invite the expected operators during a pilot, including someone who only helps during sales. Test who can understand the order, continue the conversation and complete a handover.

Review [shared versus personal handling](https://whatflow.tech/compare/shared-whatsapp-inbox-vs-personal-phones) before deciding how the new colleague joins.

## WhatFlow’s allowance and team costs

For a concrete baseline, consider 1,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Starter produces an app-side cost of $4.99 at that volume. This calculation uses its $4.99 base, 2,000 included sends and $0.005 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
