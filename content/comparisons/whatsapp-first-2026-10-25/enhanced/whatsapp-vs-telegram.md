## The short answer

For a Shopify store’s consented order conversations, choose WhatsApp with WhatFlow before commissioning a separate bot-based operating workflow. Store events, templates and the no-seat-fee inbox give the team a focused application, with all features available from Free. Telegram can remain valuable for an existing audience or defined bot requirement. That exception should follow a real customer need; global user totals do not outweigh a maintained Shopify conversation where the buyer already wants help.

## Define the private order workflow behind the bot

For a hypothetical Telegram proposal, ask the supplier to describe how the bot recognizes the intended customer, obtains an order reference and passes an unresolved question to a person. Keep a community announcement separate from that private service route. Compare the same task with WhatFlow’s Shopify WhatsApp workflow, using recipients who actually prefer each channel. An available bot interface does not by itself establish a maintained support process, but a business already serving a Telegram audience may have good reasons to keep it. Include configuration ownership and ongoing changes in the budget. Choose from the working customer journey rather than a global user-count comparison or an assumption that all bot projects are expensive.

Test an order update and a follow-up question with consenting recipients in the actual target market. Check identification, product context and handoff to another staff member.

## WhatFlow’s allowance and team costs

For a concrete baseline, consider 10,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
