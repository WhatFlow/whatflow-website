Choose WhatFlow Business API when an existing Shopify checkout and its order-related WhatsApp conversations are the operational center. ## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 20,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.

## Check what changes when a lead becomes a buyer

Hypothetical example: a prospect asks a product question through web chat, continues on WhatsApp and later places a Shopify order. In the proposed Gallabox journey, identify how the operator knows which conversation belongs to that purchase and where a delivery question is handled. Avoid assuming that a captured phone number alone resolves the correct order.

Compare the order-event and reply portion with WhatFlow Business API’s supported workflow. If the pre-purchase qualification process has its own value, keep that requirement and owner explicit rather than replacing it with an order notification. Record the identity and handoff gaps in either architecture before expanding the automation beyond the data it can establish.
