## Separate acquisition context from order support ownership

For a hypothetical Facebook campaign enquiry, note the product and offer the buyer saw, then follow the journey into an order question. Ask the team whether the customer wants to continue in Messenger or voluntarily use a WhatsApp help route. A channel change should preserve the useful context without implying that a page interaction granted WhatsApp campaign permission. WhatFlow can supply the Shopify WhatsApp layer when that is the chosen route. Keep campaign spending separate from the cost of answering the subsequent question, and document which person owns each stage. Evaluate the handover using the store’s actual audience rather than broad claims about either app’s worldwide popularity.

Test a campaign enquiry followed by an order question. Check which team handles the handoff and whether the store operator can understand the order without searching unrelated social conversations.
