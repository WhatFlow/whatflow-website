## Start with the customer’s job

A store may have quiet months followed by a launch, holiday or sale. A large fixed software commitment can be hard to justify when the operational need is stable but message volume changes sharply.

## Where each approach helps

Longer contracts can bring support, implementation or pricing benefits for predictable larger organizations. A smaller merchant should compare those benefits with flexibility and the volume it can realistically sustain.

## The practical cost difference

Model a quiet month, a normal month and a peak month using the actual allowances and overage rates. Do not assume the cheapest base subscription is the cheapest option at peak volume.

## Compare the seasonal total before choosing a commitment

Hypothetical example: a gift store expects one launch month, two busy gifting months and several quieter periods. Place each period’s recipients and follow-ups in a calendar, then apply the proposed plan and usage charges. For a fixed contract, record the full committed amount even when the month’s usage is low. For a changeable plan, use only changes the provider’s terms actually permit.

Add implementation and seasonal staffing access separately. A promotion can generate support questions after its final send, so the operating requirement does not disappear on the campaign end date. Choose the arrangement whose annual cost and maintained coverage fit the calendar, rather than extrapolating the peak month into a permanent requirement.

Estimate sends per order and campaign recipients for each period. Include extra customer questions during peaks and ensure the people helping can access the inbox without unexpected seat costs.

Include [seasonal team access](https://whatflow.tech/compare/growing-shopify-team-whatsapp-vs-seat-upgrades) when calculating the busiest period.

## WhatFlow’s allowance and team costs

For a concrete baseline, consider 5,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Plus produces an app-side cost of $9.99 at that volume. This calculation uses its $9.99 base, 5,000 included sends and $0.004 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.

Use [Meta’s pricing page](https://business.whatsapp.com/products/platform-pricing) for the destination and category assumptions in the seasonal budget.
