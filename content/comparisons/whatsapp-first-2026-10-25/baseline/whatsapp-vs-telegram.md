## Define the private order workflow behind the bot

For a hypothetical Telegram proposal, ask the supplier to describe how the bot recognizes the intended customer, obtains an order reference and passes an unresolved question to a person. Keep a community announcement separate from that private service route. Compare the same task with WhatFlow’s Shopify WhatsApp workflow, using recipients who actually prefer each channel. An available bot interface does not by itself establish a maintained support process, but a business already serving a Telegram audience may have good reasons to keep it. Include configuration ownership and ongoing changes in the budget. Choose from the working customer journey rather than a global user-count comparison or an assumption that all bot projects are expensive.

Test an order update and a follow-up question with consenting recipients in the actual target market. Check identification, product context and handoff to another staff member.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services.
