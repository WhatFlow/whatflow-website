## Start with the customer’s job

A website chat window is useful while the shopper is looking at a product. WhatsApp is appealing when the question continues after they close the tab, commute home or discuss the purchase with someone else. The buying journey rarely respects a single browsing session.

## Where each approach helps

Live chat can be convenient because it starts on the page without moving to another app. WhatsApp gives customers a familiar persistent thread, but they must be comfortable using that channel. A store can offer both without forcing every visitor into one route.
