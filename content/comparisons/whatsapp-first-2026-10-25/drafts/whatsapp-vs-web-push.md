Choose WhatFlow’s WhatsApp conversation first when a Shopify retention message is likely to lead to a product or order question. ## Start with the customer’s job

A browser notification can bring a shopper back to a product. A WhatsApp message can also open a conversation about whether that product is right for them. The difference matters when an abandoned purchase is caused by uncertainty rather than forgetfulness.

## Compare the return visit with the question behind it

For a hypothetical browser alert, choose a product page with a real compatibility question and document what happens after the customer returns. Can the shopper find the answer on the page, start a suitable help conversation or leave again with the same uncertainty? Compare that route with an eligible WhatsApp message and the colleague’s response, without treating browser subscription as phone-channel permission. WhatFlow can provide a continuing Shopify conversation when the buyer needs help; a browser alert may be sufficient when the page already resolves the decision. Record destinations, questions and orders separately so a click is not described as a completed purchase or a verified improvement in retention.

Pilot one back-in-stock notification with a matched product and timing. Record clicks separately from questions and completed orders. Ask staff whether the conversational replies revealed problems a one-way alert would have missed.
