## Key takeaways

- Use WhatFlow for Shopify updates that customers need to answer.
- Bring the order conversation and the human team together without seat fees.
- Keep email for the detailed records and content it handles well.

## Start with the customer’s job

A customer waiting for an order may want a quick answer rather than another long email. WhatsApp gives that moment a direct conversational home. The message can carry the relevant update and let the customer ask a follow-up while the store’s team sees the conversation in one place.

## Where each approach helps

Email remains excellent for detailed content, invoices, newsletters and information customers may want to search or forward later. It can also be very economical at scale. WhatsApp’s advantage is the interaction around a timely event, not a claim that email is obsolete or always ignored.

## The practical cost difference

Email platforms commonly price by contacts and sending allowances. WhatsApp involves app costs and applicable message delivery fees. Compare incremental margin and useful responses rather than treating an email open as equivalent to a WhatsApp order confirmation.

## Keep one detailed record and one useful conversation

For a hypothetical first rollout, choose a customer question that often follows a detailed receipt, such as interpreting a partial fulfillment. Keep the receipt available through the existing email program and offer an eligible WhatsApp route for clarification. Ask another colleague to answer using the order state rather than merely forwarding the original message. WhatFlow can provide that Shopify-oriented inbox without replacing a functioning newsletter system. Record whether the extra contact resolves uncertainty or simply duplicates information the buyer already has. Compare permissioned reach, useful responses and total handling effort before assigning more jobs to the channel. Neither an email open nor a WhatsApp read alone proves an improved purchase outcome.

Choose one operational message, such as a fulfillment update. Keep the content useful and the timing comparable, then review replies, delivery and resulting support effort. Leave detailed receipts in the channel that already serves them well.
