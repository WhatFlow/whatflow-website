## Key takeaways

- Make the review invitation an answerable WhatsApp follow-up.
- Give support replies an owner without adding WhatFlow inbox seats.
- The review provider controls verification, not the invitation channel.

## Start with the customer’s job

A review request works best when the customer has had a fair chance to use the product. WhatsApp can make the request feel like a natural follow-up, especially if the same conversation already contains useful order updates.

## Where each approach helps

Email remains useful for richer product context and established review programs. Neither channel should pressure the customer, imply that only positive reviews are welcome or send a request before the experience is complete.

## Keep the review destination independent of the invitation channel

For a hypothetical invitation review, use the same purchased item and intended review form while changing only the eligible invitation route. Open the destination on a phone, inspect the product association and check what the review provider will display about source and verification. A WhatsApp link does not make a review verified merely because it came from an order conversation. Include a customer who replies with a missing-part question instead of submitting feedback; that response needs support ownership without a demand for a positive rating. WhatFlow can provide the WhatsApp conversation while the review provider controls collection rules. Coordinate the email schedule before adding another reminder.

Test the timing, product link and review destination with a completed test purchase. Coordinate with the existing review platform so one delivery does not trigger several overlapping requests.
