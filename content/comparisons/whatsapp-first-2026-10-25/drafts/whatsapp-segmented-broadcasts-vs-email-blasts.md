## Compare relevance without giving email an artificial handicap

For a hypothetical launch, define the same relevant product-interest rule for the email audience and the permissioned WhatsApp audience where the available data permits it. Keep the offer, destination and reporting window clear, then record the questions that require a conversation. Comparing a carefully selected WhatsApp group with an unrelated mass email would confuse targeting with channel choice. WhatFlow’s shared inbox is useful when an eligible recipient needs help deciding between variants, but segmented email can also be valuable. Review margin, opt-outs and response handling alongside attributed orders. Use the exercise to choose a sensible division of channel work rather than to manufacture a universal winner.

Pick a specific product audience and document exclusions. Use a clear offer, a useful destination and a manageable reply volume so the team can answer interested shoppers promptly.
