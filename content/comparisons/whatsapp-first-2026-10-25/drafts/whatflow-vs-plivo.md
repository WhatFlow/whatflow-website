## Pricing: what belongs in the comparison

Plivo’s prerequisites require sales-provisioned WhatsApp access and a minimum monthly commitment of $1,000 in the US data region or ₹100,000 in India. This commitment spans Plivo products and excludes pass-through fees; it is not a setup fee. The page also announces platform-message and registered WhatsApp-account fees effective November 1, 2026. Those are future terms at this review, so confirm the rollout date, regional contract and eligible spend before budgeting.

Check the [Plivo official product and pricing information](https://www.plivo.com/docs/messaging/concepts/whatsapp/prerequisites) for the applicable package and billing term; a product capability does not establish that every plan includes it.
