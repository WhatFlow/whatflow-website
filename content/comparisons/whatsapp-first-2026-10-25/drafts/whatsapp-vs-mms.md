## Start with the customer’s job

A product image can answer a question faster than a description. For Shopify stores selling visually distinctive products, WhatsApp gives that image a conversation around it: the shopper can ask about the variant, delivery or fit in the same thread.

## Where each approach helps

MMS can carry media through a carrier-based messaging route and may reach customers who do not use WhatsApp. Availability, media handling and costs depend on the recipient’s market and service. WhatsApp needs the customer to use the app, so actual audience reach should decide the pilot.

## Evaluate the image and its reply as one task

For a hypothetical visual comparison, prepare a product image with a short explanation of the specific variant and a destination that matches it. Ask each proposed provider how the final asset is delivered in the intended markets, which chargeable unit applies and where replies are received. Do not infer MMS support or pricing from an SMS-only quote. On WhatsApp, check the eligible message format and current destination before using the product media. WhatFlow can support the Shopify conversation that follows, such as a question about a different size. Evaluate whether the customer and colleague can identify the intended item, without declaring either channel universally richer, cheaper or more reachable.

Use a product with two similar variants and send a clear image with its name and destination link. Check how easily the recipient identifies the right item and whether an operator can resolve a variant question before purchase.
