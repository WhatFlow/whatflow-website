# WhatFlow product playbooks

Five standalone guides, each with a real WhatFlow Demo Store screenshot and a cover in the existing blog style:

- [Fix failed WhatsApp broadcasts](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatflow-whatsapp-broadcast-failure-retry)
- [Handle launch replies in Team Inbox](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatflow-whatsapp-product-launch-reply-playbook)
- [Build an API-triggered Inbox workflow](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatflow-api-triggered-inbox-workflow)
- [Connect Inbox events with signed webhooks](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatflow-team-inbox-signed-webhooks)
- [Map CSV or Excel audience fields](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatflow-whatsapp-campaign-spreadsheet-mapping)

The five inbound links are recorded in crosslinks.json. The existing comparisons, bylines, covers and publication dates are unchanged. The rehearsal adds five post records and five media records, verifies the other records and foreign keys, and confirms that rerunning the SQL does not change the resulting state. All ten edited or new article bodies rendered through the site's RichText component.

The technical guides describe the available configuration and a proposed test plan; they don't claim that sample messages, live webhooks, or example workflows were executed.
