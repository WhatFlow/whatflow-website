# WhatFlow Team Inbox — capability audit, November 8, 2026

Scope: Business API's Team Inbox, not the separate linked-device app. Cross-checked the live [Inbox page](https://whatflow.tech/inbox), [release history](https://whatflow.tech/changelog), [MCP docs](https://whatflow.tech/docs/mcp), the existing demo captures, and the Inbox repository. No new sends, account changes or end-to-end tests were performed.

| Area | Verified capabilities | Copy limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue and ownership | All, Mine and Unassigned queues; Needs reply, Closing soon, Unread, Pinned, active/expired service-window and Done filters; saved filter views; claim, assign, mark done and reopen; color labels and teammate typing warnings. | Needs reply means the customer wrote last with no subsequent send. Closing soon means an open window ends within four hours. Describe saved views as per-store browser preferences, not shared team configuration. |
| Team accounts | Shopify owner launch, individual agent logins, owner-managed agent creation and password resets; internal message-linked notes; agent drafts kept separately from customer-visible messages. | No per-seat fee per public product page. Don't infer SAML, SSO for every agent, enterprise certification or a generic all-channel helpdesk. |
| Conversation productivity | Global search by customer or message and in-chat search, keyboard shortcuts, unread markers, private notes, personalized canned responses, per-chat saved drafts, read-receipt and typing settings, 24-hour window indicator. | Undo send is an optional five-second hold for text replies; it's not recall of a delivered message. Free-form messaging is subject to Meta's service-window rules. |
| Message handling | Approved templates, media and voice, contact cards, location messages, quoted replies, reactions, delivery status, send errors and retry where supported, photo viewer and multi-file downloads. Template manager supports preview, rejection details and copying approved templates. | Do not advertise every message type as sendable in every context or a delivery guarantee. |
| Shopify customer context | Contact profile with order date, total, payment/fulfillment status and tracking when available, matched using the Shopify customer phone. Shopify contact import/sync, labels and typed custom attributes for contacts and conversations. | Present as lookup, not refund, order editing or guaranteed matching. Contact sync isn't continuous Shopify segment-to-Inbox audience sync. |
| Commerce and campaigns | Catalog and product sharing, WhatsApp cart follow-up with a Shopify checkout link. Campaigns using CSV/Excel or existing conversations, field mapping, template preview/test send, scheduling, recipient-level results, exports and failed-recipient retries. | Document marketing consent and distinguish segment broadcasts initiated in Shopify Admin. The checkout link points to Shopify. |
| Automation | API-triggered Inbox workflows with messages, decisions and waits; WhatsApp Flows for in-chat forms; conversation starters and commands; signed outbound webhooks for messages, interactions, assignments, labels and workflow outcomes with test, logs and retries. | Differentiate Inbox Workflows, WhatsApp Flows and Shopify Flow; they aren't interchangeable. |
| Connected AI | Permissioned AI clients can help find conversations, draft replies, manage notes and labels, look up orders and create broadcast drafts. | AI-created broadcast drafts are not sent or scheduled by the MCP tool. Explain which reply actions require review based on the permissions and draft-mode configuration. |

Primary code references in the Inbox repository: `app/components/sidebar/InboxSidebar.tsx`, `app/routes/_app.inbox.$id.tsx`, `app/components/inbox/Composer.tsx`, `CustomerOrders.tsx`, `ContactProfileDrawer.tsx`, `app/components/settings/AccountTeamSettings.tsx`, `app/routes/_app.webhooks.tsx`, and `app/components/broadcasts/`.

Positioning: **Shopify's order context, team triage, and WhatsApp campaigns in one inbox, without per-agent fees.** The live page already shows campaigns and analytics. Add a concrete day-in-the-life demo next: filter Needs reply → claim a chat before the window closes → see the Shopify tracking link → personalize a saved reply → leave an internal note and mark done. A second demo can show a product launch broadcast and what the team does with replies and delivery failures. Both are stronger evidence than a generic shared-inbox feature list.
