## Avoid double-counting recovered revenue

Suppose a customer receives an email reminder, a WhatsApp reminder and a paid retargeting ad before completing a checkout. Ask how each system reports that sale. Then agree on the measurement rule you will use internally. Multiple dashboards can legitimately associate themselves with the same order.

For a useful pilot, record the message timestamp, the order timestamp and campaign identifiers. Compare incremental margin after software, messaging costs and discounts, rather than celebrating gross attributed revenue alone. This is an evaluation method, not a claim about either vendor's attribution implementation.

Pair attribution review with [cross-channel message coordination](https://whatflow.tech/blog/coordinate-email-sms-whatsapp-customer-messages) around the same purchase.

## A worked WhatFlow cost example

For a concrete baseline, consider 10,000 sent messages in a month. Among the listed WhatFlow plans, Growth produces an app-side cost of $29.00 at that volume. This calculation uses its $29.00 base, 20,000 included sends and $0.0025 rate above the allowance. It assumes a single monthly allowance and does not include tax or Meta delivery charges. It is a planning example, not a combined quote for every possible message category or destination.

Keep three lines in the budget: the application, applicable WhatsApp delivery fees, and any separate implementation or services. Meta pricing varies by destination and category, and some service or qualifying utility messages have different treatment. An app allowance does not override Meta’s rules. The current installation terms and rate pages should be checked before committing a campaign budget.
