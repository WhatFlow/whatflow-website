## Separate Interakt's product packages first

Interakt's [pricing page](https://www.interakt.shop/pricing/) presents Marketing & Support Hub, AI Agents and Sales CRM as different offerings. The page advertises unlimited agents, but its summary and detailed table use conflicting role descriptions. Confirm the selected package, operator permissions, billing period and message charges in writing. Do not treat unlimited logins as proof that every Sales CRM role is included.

Its [FAQ](https://www.interakt.shop/faqs/) also describes unlimited team-member logins and notes that Shopify billing uses USD while published INR prices are shown. These pages were checked October 27, 2026. It would be inaccurate to claim Interakt universally charges per inbox seat or to compare an INR card directly with a USD Shopify charge without checking the conversion and billing terms.

WhatFlow's advantage is consistent feature access across its messaging plans. You do not need a higher feature edition to use the available catalog, inbox or Shopify capabilities. Match the sending allowance to the workload using [current WhatFlow pricing](https://whatflow.tech/pricing).

## Decide whether you need store operations or a wider sales system

A merchant answering “where is my order?” needs a different process from a sales team qualifying leads through several stages. Both involve a conversation, but they create different records, responsibilities and follow-ups.

For a Shopify-led workflow, list the messages and decisions you actually need: order confirmation, a fulfillment question, a customer reply, perhaps a product recommendation. Interakt may be attractive when its separate sales or AI products solve a defined requirement. WhatFlow currently does not provide AI, so include that difference openly if AI is essential.

If your immediate need is Shopify WhatsApp, paying for a broader operating model should earn its place through work it replaces. WhatFlow keeps the buying decision focused on the messaging volume while making its available feature set accessible from Free upward.

## Use a delayed COD reply to expose the real workflow

Take a fictional COD order with two items. One has already been fulfilled when the customer asks to change the delivery address. Now add an older conversation about the customer's previous order.

The evaluation questions are concrete:

- Which order does the operator believe the customer means?
- Where can they check the current fulfillment state?
- Who decides whether the requested change is still possible?
- What does the team record before confirming the outcome?

A button click or message reply does not establish that an order changed correctly. Keep the test in an inactive workflow or written walkthrough until you have reviewed the actual supported actions. Our [order-reply Flow guide](https://whatflow.tech/blog/whatsapp-order-replies-shopify-flow-cod-confirmation) helps distinguish the message event from the operational decision.

WhatFlow's close Shopify integration is valuable here because the team can build around store events and supported Flow actions. It does not eliminate the need to check a late customer request against the present order state.

## Compare the cost of the required package

Give both providers the same brief: monthly outbound sends, destination countries, expected customer replies, support roster and any required sales or AI modules. Ask how each proposal treats usage beyond its allowance and which fees remain outside the subscription.

For WhatFlow, the roster creates no inbox seat charge. All available features remain included, with message allowances and rates varying by plan. Applicable Meta charges are separate. For Interakt, preserve its unlimited-agent terms where they apply and distinguish any separate Sales CRM role or AI package. An honest comparison can still favor WhatFlow without inventing a seat disadvantage.

## Questions worth asking

### Is WhatFlow a practical Interakt alternative for Shopify?

WhatFlow offers Shopify extensions, order-linked automation and a team inbox without moving feature access into a higher subscription. That makes it a compelling first choice when the store needs the operational journey more than a separate sales platform. Interakt deserves a look when its additional commerce or voice scope is central to the rollout, rather than an attractive demonstration you are unlikely to use.

### How should I compare Interakt pricing with WhatFlow?

Interakt offers several product packages and billing currencies. Its pricing materials advertise unlimited agents, but role wording differs between its summary and detailed table. ### Does WhatFlow charge per inbox seat?

No. WhatFlow’s team inbox has no seat fees. Adding colleagues does not create a per-user subscription; the messaging plan and actual usage still apply.

### Are WhatFlow features restricted on the free plan?

No. All plans have the same feature access. Message allowances and rates differ, and applicable Meta delivery charges are separate. Theme-matched WhatsApp buttons are free forever.
