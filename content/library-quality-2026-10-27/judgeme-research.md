## Primary-source packet for new guides

All sources below were fetched with the web tool on October 27, 2026. Findings are paraphrases, not quotations. Official documentation is authoritative for its own platform's mechanics; two independent vendors cannot independently establish another vendor's exact settings.

### Shipping exception conversations

[Shopify order tracking](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/setup/order-status-page/order-tracking) says tracking becomes available through the order status page, shipping emails and Shop after tracking information is attached to a fulfilled order. Carrier support determines tracking detail. A carrier estimate must not become a merchant guarantee.

Write a decision tree for label created/no movement, delayed in transit, delivered but not received, and split shipments. Agent verifies Shopify and carrier information, states the known facts, commits to a next update time, and escalates. WhatFlow is the conversation workspace. Automatic carrier exception detection is unverified and must not be claimed.

### Returns and exchange conversations

[Shopify creating returns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns/creating-returns) supports merchant-managed returns, return instructions, estimated refunds and exchange items in Shopify admin. [Shopify self-serve returns](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/returns/self-serve-returns) describes customer return/cancellation requests from the order status page. A request is not approval or an issued refund.

Write the information checklist, eligibility check against the actual store policy, exchange-stock check, clear handoff and customer updates. Do not claim WhatFlow itself generates labels, approves returns, collects exchange balances or issues refunds. The existing post-purchase and saved-reply guides are adjacent; link to them rather than repeating their generic content.

### Catalog-assisted product support

Catalog sharing is **live, confirmed by Safwan Adnan in this conversation**. Shopify customer-segment/audience sync is upcoming; AI is unavailable. Precise app UI steps, inventory sync behavior and catalog prerequisites require an actual app walkthrough before describing settings by name.

Write a useful support sequence: understand size/use case/budget, verify variant and stock in Shopify, share a small relevant product selection, clarify delivery/returns, and provide the appropriate Shopify product/checkout path. Do not imply that a catalog message reserves stock, processes payment or completes fulfillment. The existing pre-purchase questions and cross-sell posts are related but do not provide this concrete catalog workflow.

Meta's technical catalog guide returned HTTP 429 this run, and the platform-features page was unavailable. No exact technical catalog feature claim was inferred from those failed fetches.

### Channel rules shared by all three guides

[WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy), official redirect to whatsappbusiness.com/policy, displayed September 23, 2026 as its latest update. It confirms recipient permission for subsequent contact, honoring opt-outs, and the customer-service window opening/resetting with each user message. Free-form replies are permitted within 24 hours; outside it, approved templates are required. Template approval and future delivery are not guaranteed. The policy defers charging to the separate pricing policy; do not describe all service messages as free based on this source.

This is the controlling source for channel rules, not an independent WhatFlow feature endorsement. Detailed fee schedules are outside this research scope.
