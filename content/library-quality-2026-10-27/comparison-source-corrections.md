- **Dondy:** acquisition is confirmed by both parties. Dondy promises continuity of product, founders/team, support and accounts; the buyer describes a broader AI/enterprise roadmap. Acquisition is not evidence of price increases, instability or deteriorating support. Its free button and existing official API/Shopify inbox are real. Distinguish planned roadmap from live capabilities.

- **AiSensy:** Free Forever, chatbot add-ons and fair-use limits are documented. No blanket per-seat claim. Vendor service-message price claims must not override current Meta primary guidance.

Current pricing explicitly rejects per-agent billing; replace generic possible seat-fee language with ticket-based pricing. Its Shopify and omnichannel capabilities are genuine strengths.
