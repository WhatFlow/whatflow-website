WhatFlow connects your Shopify store to WhatsApp through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform (the Cloud API). It sends the messages your store would normally send by email (order confirmations, COD checks, shipping updates, cart reminders), and it gives your team one shared inbox for the replies.

<Note>
These docs cover **WhatFlow Business API** ([Shopify App Store listing](https://apps.shopify.com/whatflow-official-api)). WhatFlow Chat Automation is a separate app that links a phone the way WhatsApp Web does. The two apps have different setups and settings. [Compare them](/compare/whatflow-chat-automation-vs-business-api).
</Note>

## How the pieces fit together

| Piece | What it does | Where you use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shopify app** | Connects WhatsApp, turns automations on and off, edits templates, shows analytics and billing. | Shopify admin › Apps › WhatFlow |
| **Automations** | Send a message when something happens in your store: an order is placed, a checkout is abandoned, a parcel ships. | Each automation has its own page in the app |
| **Live Inbox** | A shared WhatsApp inbox where your team reads and answers customers. | Opened from the app, or signed in to directly |
| **Storefront extensions** | A WhatsApp chat button, an Order via WhatsApp button, a back-in-stock form, and an order confirmation block for the Thank you page. | Shopify theme and checkout editors |
| **Shopify Flow** | WhatsApp actions and triggers you can use in your own Flow workflows. | Shopify admin › Apps › Flow |

## What you need

- A Shopify store.
- A Facebook account to sign in to Meta during setup. You can pick an existing Meta business portfolio and WhatsApp account, or create them in the same step.
- A phone number for your business that can receive a verification code from Meta by SMS or call. A number already on the WhatsApp Business app can be connected too. See [Connect WhatsApp](/docs/getting-started/connect-whatsapp).
- An active WhatFlow plan. Nothing sends without one.
- A payment method added in Meta. Meta bills WhatsApp message charges to you directly, separately from WhatFlow.

## Every feature, every plan

All features are included on every WhatFlow plan. Plans differ in how many messages are included and the rate for extra messages. Live Inbox has no per-seat fee. See [Plans and billing](/docs/account/billing).
