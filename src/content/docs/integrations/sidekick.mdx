Sidekick is Shopify's AI assistant in Shopify admin. WhatFlow connects to it, so you can ask Sidekick about your WhatsApp setup and templates in plain language, and let it draft template wording for you.

## What Sidekick can do with WhatFlow

WhatFlow gives Sidekick two sets of abilities: **WhatFlow Tools** to look things up, and **WhatFlow Actions** to help write templates.

### Look things up (WhatFlow Tools)

Sidekick can read, but not change, this information:

| Ask about | What Sidekick sees |
| --- | --- |
| Automations | Which WhatsApp automations are on or off (order confirmation, fulfillment, abandoned checkout, back in stock, auto-replies and others). |
| WhatsApp connection | Whether WhatsApp is connected, your phone number, its quality rating, messaging limit, and any account restrictions or alerts. |
| Templates | Search your templates by keyword, automation or Meta approval status. |
| One template | The full template and its Meta review status, including why Meta rejected, paused or disabled it and how to fix it. |
| Back in stock | Customers waiting for restock alerts, by product, with pending, notified and failed counts. |
| Setup health | Every setup problem with a fix: WhatsApp connection, account restrictions, phone quality, automations turned on without an approved template, and templates Meta rejected, paused or disabled. |

Example questions:

- "Is WhatFlow set up correctly?"
- "Why was my order confirmation template rejected?"
- "Which WhatsApp automations are turned on?"
- "How many customers are waiting for back-in-stock alerts?"

### Help write templates (WhatFlow Actions)

Sidekick can open the WhatFlow template editor and draft a template with you: the wording, buttons and personalised variables. It can also switch the template's automation (trigger event) and category.

Sidekick only fills in the editor. Nothing is saved or sent to Meta until you review it and save or submit it yourself. See [Template editor](/docs/templates/editor).

## Sidekick buttons in WhatFlow

Some WhatFlow pages have buttons that start a Sidekick chat with the right question already filled in:

| Button | Where | What it asks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Generate with AI** | Template editor | Help drafting the template you are editing. |
| **Fix with Sidekick** | Templates list, on templates Meta rejected, paused or disabled | What Meta objects to and how to fix it. |
| **Explain my delivery rate** | Analytics | An explanation of your delivery rate. |
| **Ask Sidekick how to improve** | Connection, next to your quality rating | What your quality rating and messaging limit mean, and how to improve them. |

These buttons only appear when Sidekick is available in your Shopify admin.

## Troubleshooting

### The Sidekick buttons don't show

Sidekick is not available on your store or device, for example in the Shopify mobile app. Open WhatFlow in Shopify admin on a computer.

### "WhatFlow is turned off in Sidekick."

Turn WhatFlow on in Sidekick's app settings, then try again.

### Sidekick can't send messages for me

That is expected. Sidekick can look things up and draft templates, but it does not send WhatsApp messages. To let an AI app read chats and draft replies, see [AI apps (MCP)](/docs/mcp).
