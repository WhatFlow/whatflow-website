Auto-replies answer a customer's WhatsApp message straight away when it matches words you choose. Use them for questions customers ask again and again, such as "where is my order?", returns or payment options.

<Path>Home › Auto Replier</Path>

## How it works

Each auto-reply is a rule with two parts: the text to look for, and the reply to send. When a customer sends you a message, WhatFlow checks it against every rule that is on. If a rule matches, WhatFlow sends that rule's reply right away.

- Only one auto-reply is sent per customer message. If several rules match, only the rule with the lowest **Order** number replies.
- The reply is sent as a WhatsApp reply that quotes the customer's message. Emoji reactions are the only exception.
- Auto-replies are free-form replies to a customer who has just written to you, so they are always inside the [24-hour window](/docs/getting-started/whatsapp-basics). They do not need an approved template.
- Auto-replies are never stopped by the [Blocklist](/docs/account/blocklist).
- Each auto-reply that Meta accepts counts as one message toward your plan. See [Plans and billing](/docs/account/billing).

<Note>

WhatFlow auto-replies are keyword rules only. There is no welcome message, no away message and no business-hours setting. The **Opening hours** starter is an ordinary keyword rule with fixed text: it replies with the same text at any time of day, and you edit the hours in the text yourself.

</Note>

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Auto-replies">

From **Home**, open the **Auto Replier** card. The page is called **Auto-replies**.

</Step>
<Step title="Create a rule">

Click **Create auto-reply**. Under **Start from a common question**, pick a starter. Each starter fills in the words to look for and a reply you can edit. Pick **Start from scratch** to write your own.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose what to match">

Under **When a customer sends**, choose a **Match** type and enter the words or text to look for. Give the rule a **Rule name** if you like.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the reply">

Under **Reply with**, pick a **Reply type** and fill in its fields. The **Preview** shows how the message will look in WhatsApp.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Click **Save** in the bar at the top of the page. You see "Auto-reply rule created." when it is saved. New rules are on by default.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Starters

All starters use the **Any of these words** match type. You can change the words and the reply after you pick one.

| Starter | Words it looks for | Reply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Greeting** | hi, hello, hey, good morning, good evening | "Hi! 👋 Thanks for reaching out. How can we help you today?" with buttons **Track my order**, **Returns**, **Talk to us** |
| **Where's my order?** | track my order, where is my order, order status, tracking, delivery | "You can follow your order anytime from the tracking link in your shipping confirmation. Send us your order number and we'll check on it for you." |
| **Returns and exchanges** | returns, return, refund, exchange | "We're happy to help with a return or exchange. Send us your order number and the item you'd like to return, and we'll guide you through the next steps." |
| **Talk to a person** | talk to us, agent, human, support, call me | "Thanks! A member of our team will reply here as soon as possible." |
| **Opening hours** | hours, open, opening, closing, timing | "We're open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next working day." |
| **Payment options** | payment, pay, cash on delivery, cod, card | "You can pay by card at checkout, and cash on delivery is available in most areas. Let us know if you have any questions." |
| **Start from scratch** | (none) | You choose the words and write the reply. |

<Tip>

The **Greeting** buttons work together with the other starters. When a customer taps **Track my order**, **Returns** or **Talk to us**, the button text comes back to you as the customer's next message, and the matching starter answers it. Create those starters too if you use the Greeting.

</Tip>

## Match settings

These settings are in the **When a customer sends** section.

<Settings>
<Setting name="Match" value="Any of these words">
How the customer's message is compared with your text. See the match types below.
</Setting>
<Setting name="Words to look for / Text to look for / Message / Pattern">
The text to match. The label changes with the match type. Required.
</Setting>
<Setting name="Rule name" value="Empty">
Optional. Only you see this. For example, "Greeting".
</Setting>
</Settings>

### Match types

| Match | Field label | How it matches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Any of these words** | **Words to look for** | Separate words or phrases with commas. A word matches only as a whole word: "hi" matches "Hi there" but not "shipping". Works in any language and alphabet. |
| **Message contains this text** | **Text to look for** | Replies when this text appears anywhere in the message, even inside a longer word. |
| **Message is exactly this** | **Message** | Replies only when the whole message is exactly this text. |
| **Advanced pattern (regular expression)** | **Pattern** | For advanced users. A regular expression. Use `.*` to reply to every message. A pattern with a mistake in it never matches, and no error is shown. |

All match types ignore capital letters unless you turn on **Match capital letters exactly**.

### What text is checked

WhatFlow checks the text of the customer's message. It also checks the text of a button the customer tapped and the title of a menu row they picked. Photos, videos, voice notes and other media never trigger an auto-reply, even when they have a caption.

## More options

Open **More options** to see these settings.

<Settings>
<Setting name="Order" value="0">
A whole number, 0 or higher. When a message matches several rules, only the one with the lowest number replies. If two matching rules have the same number, the rule created first replies.
</Setting>
<Setting name="Match capital letters exactly" value="Off">
When on, "Help" and "help" count as different messages.
</Setting>
</Settings>

The **Status** box on the right has the switch **Rule is on**. It is on by default. Turned-off rules are kept but never reply.

## Manage your rules

The **Your auto-replies** table lists every rule, 10 per page, with a search box. The columns are **When a customer sends** (rule name and the words it looks for), **Match**, **Reply**, **Order**, **Status** (**On** or **Off**) and **Actions**.

- Click **Edit** to change a rule. Save with the bar at the top of the page, or click **Discard**. If you leave with unsaved changes, WhatFlow asks you to confirm.
- Click **Delete** to remove a rule. Messages that match it won't get an automatic reply anymore. This can't be undone.
- The **Summary** box shows how many rules are on and off.

## When auto-replies don't fire

An auto-reply is not sent when:

- The rule is off, or a rule with a lower **Order** number matched first.
- The message is an opt-out or opt-in keyword, such as STOP or START. These are handled by the [Blocklist](/docs/account/blocklist).
- The message is a [Store Report](/docs/account/store-report) request sent from one of your authorized numbers.
- The customer is answering the quick-reply buttons of an [order confirmation](/docs/automations/order-confirmation) sent in the last 3 days.
- The customer is replying to a [review request](/docs/automations/review-requests) that the review request feature handles.
- The message has no text, such as a photo or voice note.
- Your WhatFlow subscription is not active, or WhatsApp is not connected.
- Meta has restricted your WhatsApp account. When this happens, WhatFlow turns off auto-replies along with your other automations.

## Shared WhatsApp numbers

If the same WhatsApp number is connected to more than one of your stores, the page shows "This WhatsApp number is shared with your other stores, so their auto-replies also answer here." Rules from your other stores are marked **Other store**.

All active rules from every store on the shared number are checked together, and the same **Order** rule decides which one replies. Give rules on different stores different **Order** numbers so you know which one wins.

## Troubleshooting

### A customer got no reply

Check the list under "When auto-replies don't fire" above. The most common causes are a rule that is off, another rule with a lower **Order** number matching first, and a match type that is too strict.

### "hi" doesn't match "hii" or a word inside a longer word

**Any of these words** matches whole words only. Add the other spelling to the list, or use **Message contains this text**. Be careful with short text in **Message contains this text**: "hi" also matches "shipping".

### My regular expression never matches

A pattern with a mistake never matches, and WhatFlow shows no error. Test the pattern in a regular expression tester first, or use one of the simpler match types.

### The wrong rule replies

Only one rule replies to each message. Give the rule you want to win a lower **Order** number than the others.

### "Please fix the errors in the form before saving."

A required field is empty or invalid. Look for the field marked in red. Common messages are "Write the reply text.", "Select a media file for this reply.", "Add at least one button." and "Use 20 characters or fewer." (button text).

### "Failed to save rule. Please try again."

The rule could not be saved. Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support@whatflow.tech.
