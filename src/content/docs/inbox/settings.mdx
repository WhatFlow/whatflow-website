The **Settings** page in Live Inbox holds your login and team, push notifications, chat preferences and your WhatsApp number's health. This page also lists the keyboard shortcuts and explains how to install the inbox on your phone.

<Path>Live Inbox › Settings</Path>

Click **Refresh** at the top of the page to reload the latest values.

## Settings sections

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Account & team** | Your login, and for the owner, the team list. See [Team members and roles](/docs/inbox/team). |
| **Push notifications** | Alerts on this device, even when the inbox is closed. |
| **Chat behavior & read receipts** | Typing indicator and undo send. |
| **Phone number & quality rating** | Your WhatsApp number's details from Meta. |

## Chat preferences

These preferences are saved **in this browser only**. Set them again on each computer or phone you use.

<Settings>
<Setting name="Typing indicators" value="On">While you type a reply, the customer sees "typing…" in WhatsApp. Only works while the 24-hour window is open.</Setting>
<Setting name="Undo send" value="On">Holds each text reply for 5 seconds so you can undo it. Switching chats or closing the tab sends it at once. Does not apply to media or templates.</Setting>
</Settings>

## Phone number and quality rating

This section shows details Meta keeps about your WhatsApp number. You cannot change them here.

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Quality rating** | Green, yellow or red, based on how recipients reacted to your messages over the last 7 days. |
| **Messaging tier** | How many different customers you can start conversations with in 24 hours. |
| **Verified display name** | The business name customers see, with its review status. |
| **Phone number ID** and **WABA ID** | Meta's IDs for your number and WhatsApp Business account. Support may ask for these. |

Read more in [WhatsApp rules to know](/docs/getting-started/whatsapp-basics). Your business profile (description, address, hours) is not edited in Live Inbox.

## Notifications

### While the inbox is open

When a new customer message arrives and an inbox tab is open:

- The inbox plays a short sound.
- If the tab is in the background, the tab title changes to show a new message, and you get a browser notification with the sender's name if you allowed notifications for the site.
- The unread count shows in the tab title and on **Live Inbox** in the sidebar.

You also get an alert when a reply window is about to close. See [Live Inbox](/docs/inbox/overview).

### Push notifications

Push notifications reach you on a computer or phone even when the inbox is closed. Turn them on separately on each device.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Push notifications">

Go to **Settings** and find **Push notifications**.

</Step>
<Step title="Turn it on">

Click **Enable on this device** and allow notifications when the browser asks. The status changes to "On for this device."

</Step>
<Step title="Test it">

Click **Send test notification**. You see how many devices the test was sent to.

</Step>
</Steps>

Who gets a push:

| Event | Sent to |
| --- | --- |
| New customer message | The person the chat is assigned to. If it is unassigned, everyone on the team. |
| Follow-up due | The person who set the follow-up, or the chat's assignee. |

- A device does not show the push while an inbox window on that same device is open and in focus. That tab plays its sound instead.
- Each person can have up to 10 devices. Adding an 11th removes the oldest.
- Click **Disable on this device** to stop push on that device.

## Install the inbox on your phone

Live Inbox works in a phone browser. You can also add it to your home screen so it opens like an app. There is no app to download from an app store.

On **iPhone and iPad**, you must add the inbox to your Home Screen before push notifications can work:

1. Open the inbox in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open the inbox from the new icon, go to **Settings**, and turn on push notifications.

On Android and on computers, use your browser's install or "Add to Home screen" option.

If the installed app asks you to sign in, use your inbox username and password. The store owner can set one up under **Settings › Account & team**. See [Team members and roles](/docs/inbox/team).

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? anywhere in the inbox to see the shortcuts.

| Where | Keys | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anywhere | Ctrl K (⌘ K on Mac) | Search chats and pages |
| Anywhere | ? | Show keyboard shortcuts |
| Conversation list | / | Search conversations |
| Conversation list | J / K | Next or previous chat |
| Conversation list | Esc | Clear the search |
| Open chat | E | Mark done or reopen |
| Open chat | F | Search messages in this chat |
| Open chat | Enter | Send the reply |
| Open chat | Shift+Enter | New line in the reply |
| Open chat | / | Insert a canned response (in the reply box) |
| Open chat | Esc | Cancel replying to a message |
| Private note | Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on Mac) | Add the note |
| In-chat search | Enter / Shift+Enter | Older or newer match |

Single-key shortcuts (such as E, F, J and K) don't work while you are typing in a field or when a dialog is open. To turn them off, open the shortcuts dialog with ? and switch off **Enable single-key inbox shortcuts**.

## What is saved per browser

These are stored in the browser you use, not in your account. They don't follow you to another computer or phone, and your teammates don't see them:

- Typing indicators and undo send
- Single-key shortcuts on or off
- Pinned chats
- Saved views
- Unsent reply drafts
- Whether the **Reply** / **Private note** bar is hidden

Push notifications are also set per device.

## Blocklist

Everyone signed in to Live Inbox, owner or agent, can view and change the blocklist. Open it from **Opt-out blocklist** on the **Broadcasts** page, or from **Manage Blocklist** on the Live Inbox page in the WhatFlow Shopify app. It is the same list in both places.

- **Respect blocklist** turns blocking on. It is off until you turn it on. When it is on, blocked customers stop getting automated template messages: broadcasts, workflow templates and the app's automations.
- **What blocked customers stop getting** sets whether blocked customers miss only marketing templates, or marketing and utility templates.
- Replies inside the 24-hour window are never blocked.

In a chat with a blocked customer, a **Blocked** banner shows with an **Unblock** button.

See [Blocklist](/docs/account/blocklist) for the full details.

## Product catalog

To send products from a chat or use product templates, your Shopify products must be synced to a Meta catalog. The sync is on the **Meta Catalog** page in the sidebar, which is marked **Soon** while it is in early access. If **Send products** shows "No catalog yet", the catalog has not been synced.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "This browser does not support push notifications." | Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. On iPhone, add the inbox to your Home Screen first. |
| "Notifications are blocked for this site." | Click the icon next to the address bar, allow Notifications, then reload the page. |
| "No device accepted the test." | Click **Disable on this device**, then **Enable on this device** again. |
| No push while the inbox is open | Expected. A device skips the push while an inbox window on that device is open and in focus. That tab plays its sound instead. |
| Pins, saved views or preferences are missing | They are saved per browser. Set them again on this device. |
| A shortcut key does nothing | Click outside the text box or close the dialog. Check that single-key shortcuts are on. |
