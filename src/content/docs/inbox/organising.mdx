Live Inbox has tools to keep a busy inbox under control: assign chats to teammates, mark them done, tag them with labels, leave private notes, set follow-up reminders and save the filters you use most.

## Assign chats

New chats from customers are **unassigned**. Nothing is assigned automatically. Each chat is assigned to one person at a time.

| Who | How |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Use the **Assignee** dropdown above the chat to pick a teammate or **Unassigned**. The dropdown only shows when your store has more than one active team member. |
| Agent | Click **Claim chat** on an unassigned chat to take it, or **Claim and reply** in the reply box. Click **Release chat** to hand back a chat that is yours. |

- Agents can only reply to chats assigned to them. Only the owner can reassign a chat from one person to another.
- When an agent starts a new chat, it is assigned to them.
- If two agents claim the same chat at the same time, the second sees "This chat was already claimed."
- When the owner disables an agent, all of that agent's chats become unassigned.

Use the **Mine** and **Unassigned** queues at the top of the conversation list to see your chats and chats nobody has taken.

When a teammate is typing a reply in the same chat, you see their name and "is typing a reply…", so two people don't answer the same customer.

## Mark chats done

Click **Mark done** in the chat header, or press E. On a phone, swipe a chat to the left. You see "Chat marked done. It returns when the customer writes again." with an **Undo** button.

- A done chat stays done until the customer writes again. Then it reopens by itself.
- Done chats leave the **Needs reply** and **Closing soon** lists and don't trigger window-closing alerts.
- Use the **Done** status filter to see them. Click **Reopen** (or press E) to reopen one.
- The owner can mark any chat done. An agent can mark a chat done if it is theirs or unassigned.

## Labels

Labels are coloured tags you put on chats, such as "VIP", "Refund" or "Wholesale". Labels are shared across your store.

### Create labels

Only the store owner can create, edit or delete labels.

<Path>Live Inbox › Labels</Path>

<Settings>
<Setting name="Label name">1 to 50 characters. Must be unique in your store (capital letters don't count as different).</Setting>
<Setting name="Color">One of 7 colours: green, blue, purple, amber, rose, cyan or orange. Custom colours are not available.</Setting>
</Settings>

There are no default labels. Deleting a label that is in use removes it from every chat. The inbox tells you how many chats are affected first. This cannot be undone.

### Use labels

Everyone on the team can add and remove labels.

- Open the customer panel by clicking the customer's name in the chat header, then add or remove labels. A chat can have several labels.
- To label many chats at once, use the **Add label…** bulk action.
- Filter the list with **Label**. Choose **Unlabeled** to find chats with no label.

Labels belong to the chat, not to the customer.

## Private notes

Private notes are messages for your team. They are never sent to the customer.

To add one, switch the reply box from **Reply** to **Private note**, type your note and click **Add note** (or press Ctrl+Enter, ⌘+Enter on Mac). Enter adds a new line. To write a note about a particular message, hover over it and choose **Add private note**.

- Notes show in the chat as amber cards with the author and time.
- Notes are text only, up to 4,096 characters. You can't edit or delete a note, attach files or mention a teammate.
- Notes work when the 24-hour window is closed and when Live Inbox chat is turned off.
- Notes don't change the unread count or the last message preview.
- Everyone on the team can read and add notes on any chat.

## Custom attributes

Custom attributes are your own fields, such as "Customer type" or "Preferred size", that you fill in for each customer or chat.

### Create attributes

Only the store owner can create attributes.

<Path>Live Inbox › Custom Attributes</Path>

<Settings>
<Setting name="Name">1 to 60 characters.</Setting>
<Setting name="Key">1 to 50 characters. Lowercase letters, numbers and underscores, starting with a letter. Cannot be changed later.</Setting>
<Setting name="Description">Optional, up to 256 characters.</Setting>
<Setting name="Scope">**Contact** saves one value for the customer's phone number. **Conversation** saves a value for this chat only. Cannot be changed later.</Setting>
<Setting name="Type">Text (up to 2,000 characters), number, date, checkbox (Yes/No) or dropdown (one choice from 1 to 50 options of up to 80 characters each). Cannot be changed later.</Setting>
</Settings>

You can't remove a dropdown option that is in use. Deleting an attribute also deletes all its values. The inbox shows how many values will be deleted first.

### Fill in values

Everyone on the team can fill in values. Open the customer panel and edit the fields under **Custom attributes**, then save. If you switch tabs in the panel with unsaved changes, the inbox warns you first.

Custom attributes stay in Live Inbox. They are not sent to Shopify or Meta.

## Follow-ups

A follow-up is a reminder to come back to a chat later.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the follow-up menu">

In the chat header, click **Follow up** (the bell).

</Step>
<Step title="Pick a time">

Choose **In 1 hour**, **In 3 hours**, **Tomorrow 9:00**, **In 3 days** or **Next Monday**. Or pick your own date and time and click **Set**. You can add a short note of up to 200 characters.

</Step>
</Steps>

- Each chat can have one follow-up. It must be in the future and no more than one year ahead.
- The chat shows a follow-up badge in the list. It turns red when the follow-up is due.
- When it is due, the person who set it (or the chat's assignee) gets a push notification if push notifications are on for their device. See [Inbox settings and shortcuts](/docs/inbox/settings).
- Click **Mark follow-up done** to clear it.
- Use the **Follow-ups** status filter to see all chats with a follow-up, sorted by due time.

## The Today page

**Today** in the sidebar shows what needs attention now: due follow-ups, customers waiting for a reply, reply windows about to close, and today's new chats.

Switch between **Everyone** and **Mine** at the top. The summary shows counts for **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Needs reply** and **New today**.

| Section | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Overdue follow-ups** | Follow-ups whose time has passed |
| **Follow-ups due today** | Follow-ups due later today |
| **Needs reply** | Customers waiting for an answer |
| **24h window closing** | Chats whose reply window is about to close |
| **New chats today** | Chats that started today |
| **From ads today** | Today's chats that came from a click-to-WhatsApp ad |
| **Coming up (7 days)** | Follow-ups in the next week |

Each section shows up to 50 chats. Click **Done** to clear a follow-up. The page uses your own time zone and updates every minute.

## Bulk actions

Click **Select conversations** (the checklist icon) at the top of the list, or tick a chat, to select several chats. Click **Select** and the count of loaded chats to select every chat that is loaded.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Mark done** | Marks the chats done. |
| **Mark read** | Clears the unread count. |
| **Assign to…** | Owner only. Assigns the chats to a teammate or **Unassigned**. |
| **Assign to me** | Agents only. Claims the chats. |
| **Add label…** | Adds a label to the chats. |

When it finishes, you see how many chats were updated, for example "Marked done: 12 chats updated." If some fail, they stay selected and the message shows the first error. Click **Cancel** to leave select mode.

## Saved views

When you change the filters at the top of the list, **Save this view** appears. Click it to save the current queue, status, label and search as a chip you can click later.

- You can save up to **8 views**.
- The name is made from the filters, for example "Mine · Needs reply · VIP".
- Click × on a chip to remove the view.
- Saved views are stored **in this browser only**. They are not shared with your team and don't appear on your other devices.

The list also remembers your filters and scroll position while the browser tab stays open.

## Pins

Pin a chat to keep it at the top of the list. Click the pin button on the chat in the list (**Pin conversation**), and click it again to unpin. On a phone, you can also swipe a chat to the right to pin or unpin it. Use the **Pinned** status filter to see only pinned chats.

Pins are stored **in this browser only**. Your teammates don't see your pins, and your pins don't appear on your other devices.
