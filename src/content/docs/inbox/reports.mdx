The **Reports** page shows how fast customers get a reply, who on your team is answering, when customers write, and which click-to-WhatsApp ads bring in conversations. Everyone on the team can open it.

<Path>Live Inbox › Reports</Path>

## Choose a date range

Pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default) or **Last 90 days**. Or pick **Custom**, choose a start and end date, and click **Apply**. A custom range can be up to 92 days.

Days and times use your browser's time zone. Reports cover your current WhatsApp number only.

<Note>

Live Inbox deletes messages after 30 days. Ranges that reach further back than 30 days can show lower numbers, because older messages are no longer stored.

</Note>

## First response time

A customer turn starts when a customer writes after you last wrote. It is answered by the first reply from a person, either in Live Inbox or in the WhatsApp Business phone app. Automated and AI messages do not count as an answer.

This section shows the median and the slowest 10% (p90) first response time, and the share of turns answered **Within 5 minutes**, **Within 1 hour** and **Within 24 hours**.

## Team

One row per sender:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sender** | The teammate or other sender |
| **Replies** | Messages they sent |
| **Chats replied to** | Chats they sent at least one message in |
| **First responses** | Customer turns they answered first |
| **Median first response** | Their typical first response time |
| **Assigned now** | Chats assigned to them right now |

Senders are grouped by type:

- **Teammate**: a person replying in Live Inbox.
- **Phone app**: replies sent from the WhatsApp Business phone app on the same number.
- **Automation**: workflows, broadcasts, broadcast tests and WhatFlow automations.
- **AI / MCP**: messages from a connected AI app, shown as the app's name followed by "(AI)". See [AI apps (MCP)](/docs/mcp).
- **Removed teammates**: messages from senders who are no longer linked to a team member.

Per-teammate done counts are not shown, because marking a chat done does not record who did it. You can download the table as a CSV file.

## Ad attribution

When a customer taps a click-to-WhatsApp ad or a boosted post on Facebook or Instagram, Meta tells WhatFlow which ad they came from. Live Inbox saves this on the chat.

You can see ad chats in several places:

- The conversation list shows **Ad ·** and the ad headline on the chat.
- The **From ads** status filter lists only ad chats.
- The customer panel shows "Came from a click-to-WhatsApp ad" (or "a boosted post"), the ad headline and text, the ad ID, the first click time, and **Open ad**.
- The **Today** page has a **From ads today** section.
- Workflows can start only on messages from ads. See [Workflows, Flows and webhooks](/docs/inbox/automation).

The first ad a customer came from stays on the chat.

### Click-to-WhatsApp ads report

The **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** section on the Reports page lists your top 50 ads in the range:

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversations** | Chats that started from this ad |
| **Got a reply** | How many of those chats got a reply from a person |
| **Median first response** | Typical time from the ad click to the first reply from a person |

If you see "No conversations from ads in this range.", no chat in the range started from an ad. You can download this table as a CSV file.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Numbers look low for a long range | Messages older than 30 days are deleted, so older periods undercount. Use a range within the last 30 days. |
| A teammate's done count is missing | Done counts per teammate are not available. The **Marked done** tile shows the store total. |
| Replies from the phone are not under a teammate's name | Replies sent from the WhatsApp Business phone app are counted as **Phone app**. |
| An ad chat has no ad details | Meta only sends ad details on the message the customer sends from the ad. Chats that started another way have none. |
