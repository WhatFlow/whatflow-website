The **Analytics** page shows how your WhatsApp messages perform: how many were sent, delivered and read, which templates and buttons customers use, what Meta may charge, and how much revenue your recovery messages brought back.

<Path>Analytics</Path>

## How it works

Analytics is in the left menu of the app. The page has eight tabs. Most tabs read their numbers from Meta. **WhatsApp Performance** and **Recovery Revenue** read from Shopify Analytics, and **Message Usage** shows the messages counted for your WhatFlow plan.

### Date range and granularity

- Choose a date range of 1 to 366 days. If the range is not valid, you see "Choose a valid date range of 1–366 days."
- On the **Messaging**, **Conversations** and **Pricing & Costs** tabs, **Granularity** sets how the numbers are grouped: **Daily**, **Monthly** or **30 Minutes (Intraday)**.
- **Recovery Revenue** has no date picker. It always shows the last 90 days.

### Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sent** | Meta accepted the message. |
| **Delivered** | Meta reported that the message reached the customer's phone. |
| **Read** | Meta reported that the customer opened it. Customers who turned off read receipts never count as read. |
| **Delivery rate** | Delivered ÷ sent. |
| **Costs** | Estimates only. Meta bills you directly for its charges. |

<Tip>

Click **Explain my delivery rate** at the top of the page to ask Shopify Sidekick about your numbers. See [Sidekick](/docs/integrations/sidekick).

</Tip>

## Overview

Message volume and delivery health for your store, from Meta.

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Sent** | Messages sent in the selected dates. |
| **Total Delivered** | Messages delivered in the selected dates. |
| **Delivery Rate** | Delivered ÷ sent. |
| **Daily Average** | Messages sent ÷ the number of days that have data. |

The **Message volume trend** chart compares sent and delivered messages over time.

## WhatsApp Performance

Delivery, engagement, failures and automations, reported in Shopify Analytics. This tab only works inside Shopify admin.

KPIs: **Messages sent**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Button replies**, **Failed** and **Unsubscribes** (customers who opted out with STOP or another opt-out keyword).

It also has:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Message funnel** | How messages move from sent to delivered, read and replied, by day. |
| **Messages by automation** | Which automation sent the messages, such as order confirmation or broadcasts. |
| **Button replies by automation** | Button taps per automation. |
| **Top templates** and **Most read templates** | Your best-performing templates. |
| **Top buttons** | The buttons customers tap most. |
| **Automations vs Inbox** | Automated messages compared with messages sent from Live Inbox. |
| **Message types** | Marketing, utility, authentication and service messages. |
| **Why messages failed** | The most common failure reasons. |
| **Paid vs free messages** | Messages Meta charges for and free ones. |
| **Content types** | The kinds of content you sent. |
| **Top countries** | The countries your messages went to. |

Good to know:

- Reporting starts from the day these events were turned on for your store, so older messages don't appear.
- **Button replies** count quick-reply buttons only, because WhatsApp doesn't report link button taps.
- Breakdowns cover the last 30 days at most.

## Message Usage

The messages counted toward your WhatFlow plan in the selected dates. It shows **Total Sent** and a table of **Date (UTC)** and **Messages**. Use this tab to check your usage against your plan. See [Plans and billing](/docs/account/billing) for what counts.

## Template Performance

Delivery, read rates and button clicks per template and per automation.

| KPI | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Template Messages Sent** | Template messages sent across all active templates. |
| **Delivered** | Template messages delivered. |
| **Read by Customers** | Read ÷ delivered. |
| **Button Clicks (CTA)** | Button clicks and the click-through rate. |

**Performance by Automation Type** breaks the numbers down by automation.

### Turn on template insights

This tab needs Meta's template insights, which are off until you turn them on. If they are off, the tab shows **One-time Setup: Enable Template Insights in WhatsApp Manager**.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open WhatsApp Manager">

Click **Open WhatsApp Manager Insights**.

</Step>
<Step title="Turn on insights">

In WhatsApp Manager, go to **Account Tools › Insights** and turn on template insights.

</Step>
<Step title="Come back later">

Template data appears on this tab once Meta has collected it. It may take some time after you turn insights on.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Recovery Revenue

Orders and revenue recovered by your WhatsApp [abandoned checkout](/docs/recovery/abandoned-checkout) and [draft order](/docs/recovery/draft-orders) messages, for the last 90 days.

Cards: **Recovered Orders**, **Recovered Revenue**, **Avg. Order Value**, **Recovered Orders by Channel**, **Recovered Revenue by Channel**, **Recovery Revenue Over Time**, **New vs Returning Customers** and **Abandoned Checkout: Revenue by Step**.

### How recovery revenue is attributed

WhatFlow adds tracking tags to the links in its messages (`utm_source=whatsapp` and `utm_campaign=whatflow`). When a customer clicks the link and places an order, Shopify's marketing reports credit the order to that link. This tab shows those orders from Shopify Analytics.

- Only orders that Shopify attributes to a WhatFlow link are counted.
- New orders may take up to 24 hours to appear.

## Troubleshooting

### Template Performance is empty

Template insights are not on in WhatsApp Manager. Follow the steps under "Turn on template insights" above. Data can take some time to appear after you turn them on.

### WhatsApp Performance shows nothing

This tab only works inside Shopify admin, and only counts messages from the day reporting was turned on for your store. Open WhatFlow from Shopify admin.

### The numbers on different tabs don't match

The tabs use different sources. **Overview**, **Messaging**, **Conversations**, **Pricing & Costs** and **Template Performance** come from Meta. **WhatsApp Performance** and **Recovery Revenue** come from Shopify Analytics. **Message Usage** counts messages for your WhatFlow plan, which uses UTC dates. Small differences between them are normal.

### Recent recovered orders are missing

Shopify can take up to 24 hours to attribute new orders. Check again the next day.
