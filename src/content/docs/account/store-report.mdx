Store Report sends you a summary of your store's sales on WhatsApp. Send a keyword such as "report" from your own phone to your connected WhatsApp number, and WhatFlow replies with today's sales, top products and more, straight from Shopify Analytics.

<Path>Home › Store Report</Path>

## How it works

1. You turn on Store Report and add the phone numbers that may ask for reports.
2. From one of those numbers, you send the keyword to your WhatFlow-connected WhatsApp number.
3. WhatFlow reads the numbers from Shopify Analytics and replies with the report.

Reports are replies to your own message, so they don't need a template and Meta doesn't charge for them. They also don't count toward your WhatFlow plan.

Only your authorized numbers get a report. Anyone else who sends the keyword gets your normal [auto-replies](/docs/conversations/auto-replies).

## Set it up

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Store Report">

From **Home**, click **Set Up Reports** on the **Store Report** card.

</Step>
<Step title="Add your numbers">

Under **Authorized numbers**, type a phone number with the country code in **Add phone number** and click **Add number**. Add up to 10 numbers.

</Step>
<Step title="Turn it on">

Under **Settings**, tick **Turn on Store Report**. Change the **Keyword** if you want.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose the sections">

Under **Report sections**, pick what the report includes.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Click **Save** in the bar at the top of the page.

</Step>
<Step title="Try it">

From an authorized phone, send the keyword (for example `report`) to your connected WhatsApp number.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Settings

<Settings>
<Setting name="Turn on Store Report">
Only the numbers below get a report. Anyone else who sends the keyword gets your normal auto-replies. You need at least one authorized number to turn it on.
</Setting>
<Setting name="Keyword" value="report">
The word that asks for a report. 2 to 20 letters or numbers. It can't be STOP. The whole message must be the keyword, optionally followed by a period. Letter case doesn't matter.
</Setting>
<Setting name="Authorized numbers">
The phone numbers that may get reports. Include the country code. Up to 10 numbers. Your connected WhatsApp number can't be one of them.
</Setting>
<Setting name="PIN (optional)">
4 to 8 digits. With a PIN, send it after the keyword, for example `report 4821`. This protects your reports if a phone is lost or shared. Click **Remove PIN** to stop using one.
</Setting>
</Settings>

### Report sections

By default the report includes **Sales**, **Top products** and **WhatsApp recovery**.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Sales** | Net sales, orders and average order value, compared with the previous period. |
| **Top products** | Your five best-selling products by net sales. |
| **Returns and discounts** | Money returned to customers and taken off by discounts. |
| **Online store traffic** | Sessions and conversion rate, compared with the previous period. |
| **WhatsApp recovery** | Orders and sales from WhatFlow recovery links. |

Choose at least one section.

## Ask for a report

Send the keyword on its own for today's report, or add a period after it:

| Send | Period |
| --- | --- |
| `report` or `report today` | **Today** |
| `report yesterday` | **Yesterday** |
| `report week`, `report 7d` or `report last week` | **Last 7 days** |
| `report month`, `report 30d` or `report last month` | **Last 30 days** |

If you set a PIN, add it at the end: `report week 4821`.

Percentages compare with the period before. For example, **Today** compares with yesterday, and **Last 7 days** compares with the 7 days before.

## What you see

The report arrives as a plain WhatsApp message, with your shop name, the period and the time in your store's time zone. For example:

<Preview>
\*My Store · Today\*

\*Sales\*
Net sales: $1,240 (+12%)
Orders: 18 (+5.9%)
Average order: $68.89 (+5.8%)

\*Top products\*
1. Linen shirt — $420
2. Canvas tote — $180

\*WhatsApp recovery\*
Recovered: 3 orders · $210

\_% compares with yesterday.\_

Send \*report yesterday\*, \*report week\* or \*report month\* for other periods.
</Preview>

If a section can't be loaded from Shopify Analytics, it shows "Not available right now." instead.

### Preview a report in the app

Under **Preview**, choose a **Period** and click **Preview report** to see the report without sending a message. If some sections can't load, the preview tells you how many.

## Limits

- One report every 30 seconds, and up to 50 reports a day, per store. Requests over the limit get no reply.
- A report is at most 4,096 characters. Longer reports are cut short.
- Up to 10 authorized numbers.

## Store Report and auto-replies

If your keyword is also used by an auto-reply, the page shows **Some auto-replies use this keyword**. Messages from authorized numbers get the report instead of those auto-replies. Other customers still get the auto-reply.

## Troubleshooting

### I sent the keyword and got no reply

- Check that **Turn on Store Report** is ticked and saved.
- Check that you sent it from an authorized number, to your connected WhatsApp number.
- The message must be only the keyword, an optional period word and an optional PIN. "Send me the report" does not work.
- You may have hit the limit of one report every 30 seconds or 50 a day. Wait and try again.

### "Incorrect PIN. Send the report keyword followed by your PIN."

The PIN is missing or wrong. Send the keyword, then a space, then your PIN.

### A section says "Not available right now."

WhatFlow could not load that section from Shopify Analytics. Try again later.

### I got an auto-reply instead of a report

The number you sent from is not in **Authorized numbers**, or Store Report is off. Add the number with its country code, then save.
